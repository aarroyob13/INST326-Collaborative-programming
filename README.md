# INST326-Collaborative-programming

Our game is called Count Down and it is based around a bomb where players must 
complete the bomb's component to disable the bomb.  The components are based
around random math equations that the player must complete.  As the round
progress, the math equation becomes harder.

Each component will have a 30 second count down, but this setting can be 
customized by the player.  If the time limits runs out, the current player is
penalize (losing health) while the opponents are given the opportunity to
complete more components.  Our group is still deciding on more penalties
for falling to disable a component within the time limit.

A scoreboard will be present keeping track of each players health, total 
disabled components, and totoal points.  Total points will be based around
the following: The total amount of disabled components, the amount of health
the player has remaining, and their ranking.  For example: the player in 
first place will receive more points compare to the player who was 
eliminated first.
